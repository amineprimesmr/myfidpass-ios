MyFidpass is the mobile app for merchants who want to launch or run a modern loyalty program — no paper cards required.

Design a branded card (logo, colors, rewards). Customers add it to Google Wallet or Apple Wallet. You manage everything from your phone.

KEY FEATURES

• Digital loyalty card — points or stamp mode, fully customizable
• QR scanner — credit points or stamps in-store in seconds
• Members — list, search, history and segmentation
• Dashboard — stats and program activity at a glance
• Campaigns — push notifications to re-engage customers
• Multi-location — manage several businesses on one account
• Google Wallet — preview and share your store’s loyalty card

WHO IS IT FOR?

Restaurants, cafés, salons, shops and local retailers who want simple loyalty without expensive hardware.

SUBSCRIPTION

Free to download. MyFidpass Pro (advanced features) is purchased via secure checkout on myfidpass.fr (Stripe). Price and billing period are shown before payment. Not sold through Google Play Billing.
